New `src/index-progress.ts` (pure): `IndexProgressEvent`, `IndexProgressEmitter` (try/catch per listener), `progressLabel`. `SearchOrchestrator` gains `private readonly progress` + `onIndexProgress(listener)`; `embedAndCommitFiles` computes notes/images totals up front and funnels all four commit sites through a single `recordCommit(path)` closure (zero bare `filesCommitted++`), emitting typed events at pass-start/paused/cadence/end; `ocrPrepass` emits an 'ocr' event per image. `main.ts` adds a one-line `onIndexProgress` passthrough. Settings tab drops the `Indexed N files` regex, subscribes, unsubscribes in .then/.catch and hide(). Local emit closure named `emitTypedProgress` to avoid colliding with the pre-existing NDJSON `this.emitProgress`. String onProgress channel untouched (logs/toasts/e2e). Tests: index-progress.test.ts + index-progress-wiring.test.ts (Scenario). Part 1/3 of plan nid_07petn152dbm3y13beujob1z3_e.
