What shipped:
- Frozen committed subset of BEIR CQADupstack-android (150 docs, 30 aggregate queries + qrels) generated by scripts/build-e2e-dataset.mjs; pin test e2e/datasets/cqadupstack-android.test.ts guards its invariants in plain npm test.
- e2e/harness/{run.mjs,page.ts} + e2e/metrics.ts: index once through LocalEmbedder->transformers.js in a real Chromium page (reusing the bench harness Chromium + .bench-cache model profile), run every query, score nDCG@10/Recall@10/MRR@10; aggregate gate vs e2e/datasets/cqadupstack-android/baseline.json with TOLERANCE 0.02.
- 10 hand-curated must-pass queries (e2e/datasets/cqadupstack-android/curated-queries.json): 5 keyword (rare unique term -> rank 1) + 5 semantic (zero content-word-overlap paraphrase -> rank <=3), one it per query. Written by reading the corpus, not tuned; one reasonable semantic miss kept out and filed as nid_ijete79awhl83gioovjhb4quk_e.
- release.sh verify_basics() now runs the e2e gate after Build, with an up-front plain-language Chromium-resolvable check mirroring scripts/bench.mjs printLaunchInfo.
- Gated on E2E=1 (not in npm test); shares .bench-cache with the bench so they cannot run concurrently. Measured ~34 s / 150 chunks on the reference container, under the 60 s budget.
- No CI job yet by design; follow-up nid_xe2pqp2t53ge36dnyc4zjkrmb_e (need-human) proposes one.
