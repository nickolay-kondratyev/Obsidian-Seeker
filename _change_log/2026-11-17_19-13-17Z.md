- Runs inside the layout-ready boot work, under the write lock, on desktop and mobile, batched (src/legacy-sidecar-migration.ts).
- Re-publishes the full own sidecar from IDB under `<deviceId>-<vaultKey>`, verifies by read-back, only then deletes this device's legacy un-keyed sidecar files.
- Skipped when the index can't vouch for itself: empty, stale, quarantined, or >50 notes unrecorded.
- Fixes the phantom-peer "syncing" banner and whole-vault re-chunk churn caused by legacy un-keyed files.
- Legacy BM25 blob is dropped, not migrated; follow-up nid_s4h30wnw7qfkrj638iplwdacn_e has desktop re-publish the keyed cross-device BM25 artifact.
