Annual general meeting, held at the community hall since the clubhouse can't fit everyone.

Attendees: around forty members, the full outgoing committee, and two prospective committee candidates standing for election, plus a handful of guests curious about joining next season.

## Agenda

Treasurer's report, committee elections, a vote on next year's membership fee, a brief update on the shed and website projects, and an open floor for any other business before the meeting formally closed for the evening.

## Decisions

Membership fee stays unchanged at £30 for the year, passed by a clear show of hands with no dissent. Priyam re-elected as chair unopposed; Dana stepped down as treasurer after three years and was thanked warmly by the whole room, with Kofi elected as her replacement by a comfortable margin.

## Discussion

The treasurer's report showed the club finishing the year with a modest surplus, helped by strong turnout at the charity ride and lower than expected insurance costs. Several members asked about the storage shed spend, wanting reassurance it wouldn't eat into next year's event budget; Dana confirmed the shed was funded separately from surplus fees. There was also a discussion about whether the club should formally affiliate with the national cycling federation; no vote was taken, and it was referred to the new committee for further research.
