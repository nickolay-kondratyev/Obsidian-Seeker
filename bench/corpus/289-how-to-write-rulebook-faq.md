# How to Write a Rulebook FAQ Entry

A good FAQ entry answers one real confusion a playtester actually hit, in plain language, without restating the whole rule from scratch or adding new exceptions.

## Start From a Real Question

Only add an entry after someone genuinely asked it during a session, ideally quoted close to verbatim, referenced back to the specific session notes, such as [[286-tidewatch-rulebook-review-meeting]], so it stays grounded in something real rather than a hypothetical confusion.

## Keep the Question Short

Phrase it the way a player would ask aloud at the table, not like a lawyer.

## One Clear Answer, No New Rules

Give exactly one clear answer, no branching "it depends" unless genuinely unavoidable. An entry should clarify existing text; never introduce a rule that isn't already implied somewhere in the rulebook, since that's a rules change disguised as clarification, and those need their own review process entirely, not a quiet FAQ addition slipped in unreviewed.

## Point Back to the Rule

Reference the specific page or section the entry clarifies, so players can find the original context instead of treating the FAQ as a standalone source that drifts over time.

## Test It Blind

Show the entry to someone who wasn't at the original session and confirm it resolves their confusion too, not just the original asker's case, since an entry that only makes sense with backstory already in your head isn't doing its job.

## Keep a Running List

Track candidate entries as they surface during playtests rather than writing the whole FAQ at once.

## Review Before Print

Kofi does a final FAQ pass before layout lock, cross-checking every entry against the current rulebook draft to catch any that reference wording that's since changed, since a stale FAQ entry is worse than no entry at all and actively misleads a confused player looking for a fast, trustworthy answer during an actual game, not a historical snapshot of a rule that used to exist in an earlier draft before the sequencing rewrite and other recent revisions.
