Appliance selection meeting, showroom visit with the kitchen designer, ninety minutes on a Saturday morning.

## Agenda

Narrow the range, refrigerator, and dishwasher shortlist down to final picks, and confirm sizing works cleanly with the cabinet order already placed.

## Range Discussion

We compared a 30-inch gas range against an induction model Priyam has been quietly lobbying for since a friend's kitchen sold her on faster boil times and easier cleanup between the two options we walked through together.

## Fridge and Dishwasher

The counter-depth fridge fits the layout better than a standard-depth model, worth the higher price given how the kitchen island sits close enough that a deeper fridge would have blocked comfortable walkway clearance. Dishwasher is a straightforward quiet model, nothing controversial there, chosen mostly on noise rating and a five-year track record the designer vouched for from other recent installs.

## Decision

We're going with induction after all, largely on Priyam's case for it plus the designer confirming our current cabinet order already accounts for the tighter clearance induction ranges typically need compared to gas, which would have meant a change order if we'd gone the other way. Counter-depth fridge and the quiet dishwasher both get ordered this week, timed to arrive alongside the cabinets so nothing sits waiting in the garage for weeks, taking up space we don't currently have to spare with the renovation still very much underway around us.

## Next Steps

Designer submits the final order Monday, confirms delivery windows by end of week, and flags anything back-ordered to us immediately, no delays.
