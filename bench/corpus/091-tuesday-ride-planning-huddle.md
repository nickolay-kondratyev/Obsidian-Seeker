Tuesday evening ride planning huddle at the clubhouse, quick and informal before the shop closed.

Attendees: the ride captain, the routes coordinator, and two volunteer marshals. Priyam chaired since Dana was out sick. Kofi took notes on his phone.

## Agenda

Three items: confirm the Saturday gravel loop, decide on wet-weather backup routes for the beginner group, and settle who is bringing the spare tubes and the first-aid kit to Sunday's social ride at Reservoir Park.

## Decisions

The Saturday gravel loop stays as planned via [[092-gravel-loop-mapping-project]] unless rain hits before Friday, in which case the group defaults to the paved rail-trail out-and-back. Lotte volunteered to carry the first-aid kit again, and Tomasz will bring three spare tubes plus a mini pump since his floor pump won't fit in a jersey pocket.

## Discussion

There was back and forth about whether the beginner group should be capped at fifteen riders. Priyam argued that beyond fifteen the marshals can't watch the back of the group, especially on the descent near the old quarry where the road narrows. Kofi felt turning people away feels unwelcoming for a club trying to grow, and suggested splitting into two pods with staggered start times instead. Nobody committed to a hard cap tonight, so it stays a soft guideline, to be revisited once we see Sunday's sign-up numbers.
