# Tidewatch Component List

Working inventory of everything in the box for the first print run. See [[272-tidewatch-project-brief]] for the scope this list is built against, flag any change here to Tomasz before a proof gets quoted, since quantities feed directly into per-unit pricing.

## Board Tiles

Six double-sided island tiles form a modular coastline that changes layout each session, printed on the same stock as the storm cards for visual consistency across the box. Priyam's tile art uses a shared color key so tide depth reads clearly at a glance, even for brand-new players who haven't learned the symbols yet, useful at convention tables where teaching time is short.

## Storm Deck

Twelve cards, up from the ten originally budgeted, following Dana's deck-size experiments this spring. Each card carries a minimum and maximum advance value plus Priyam's texture-graded illustration, which gives players an unconscious read on danger before they even flip the card over. Final count is locked pending the two-player variant test results due back this weekend; see [[273-tidewatch-design-journal-storm-mechanic]] for the reasoning behind the change and why it matters for overall pacing across both player counts, not just the four-player sessions most testing has covered so far.

## Tokens

Wind tokens (twenty, small round chits), tide markers (four, one per warden color), and flood markers (twelve, down from fourteen after the pacing fix agreed at [[271-tidewatch-weekly-sync]]). All tokens share one punch-board sheet, keeping manufacturing simple.

## Role Cards

Four warden role cards, one per player, double-sided with the core ability on the front and edge-case reminders on the back for quick reference during play. Kofi insisted on keeping every exception to a single card per role rather than spreading them across the rulebook, which has made teaching noticeably faster in recent external sessions, according to his running notes.

## Rulebook

Twelve-page saddle-stitched booklet plus a separate two-page quick reference card meant to stay on the table during play so nobody has to flip through the full booklet mid-round. Kofi owns the rulebook text and structure; Priyam handles layout and diagrams once wording is locked and Dana has signed off on the final draft for print, a process that's taken three full passes so far and at least one more expected before the proof deadline arrives next month.

## Box and Insert

Standard tuck box for the tile stack, plus a custom insert with trays for tokens, cards, and the rulebook. Tomasz confirmed the insert fits standard tooling, no custom die-cut fee. Shipping weight remains soft, tracked in [[276-tidewatch-print-run-kickoff-meeting]].
