# Tidewatch Project Brief

Tidewatch is a cooperative board game for two to four players who take on the roles of lighthouse keepers and coastal wardens defending a chain of small islands from a rising tide and an approaching storm front. Players share a single win or lose condition: either the whole crew evacuates the islanders in time, or the tide claims the map. The game targets a forty-five to sixty minute playtime and a small self-published print run rather than a mass-market release.

## Goals

The core design goal is a cooperative game that stays tense without becoming a solved puzzle, where one player quietly plays optimally while the rest watch. Dana's early prototypes leaned too hard on a single "captain" role who told everyone what to do, so the redesign spreads decision-making across four asymmetric warden roles, each holding partial information about the storm's path. The team also wants a rulebook a new group can learn from a single read-through, without a teaching video, since most sales will come from board game shops rather than in-person demos. Component count needs to stay low enough for a run of a few hundred copies to be affordable, and art needs to read clearly at small token size. See [[277-tidewatch-component-list]] for the current count.

## Scope

In scope for the first print run: four warden roles, a modular island board built from six double-sided tiles, a storm-track deck, tide markers, and a rulebook with a two-page quick reference. Out of scope for now: a solo mode, an expansion module with a fifth role, and any digital companion app, all logged as future ideas rather than commitments for this run. The team is deliberately not chasing a large-format Kickstarter with stretch goals; Lotte's plan in [[292-tidewatch-kickstarter-prep]] treats the campaign mainly as a pre-order mechanism for a run the team can already afford to print, with modest stretch goals limited to component upgrades rather than new mechanics or extra roles. Rules complexity is capped at what Kofi calls "one page of exceptions," meaning no more than a handful of role-specific rule cards beyond the core rulebook, so brand-brand-new players aren't ever overwhelmed before their first session ends, and returning groups don't need to relearn anything meaningful between plays or reread the whole booklet from the start.


## Roles

Dana leads design and owns the rulebook text, making final calls on any rule that touches balance. Priyam illustrates the board, cards, and token art. Kofi edits rules for clarity and runs most of the external playtests. Lotte handles marketing copy, the Kickstarter page, and print logistics. Tomasz playtests weekly and liaises directly with the print vendor.


## Timeline

The team is targeting a Kickstarter launch in early 2027, with a finished rulebook and locked component list by the end of 2026. Print proofs are expected in two rounds: an early proof for component fit and color accuracy, then a final proof for the approved production files, roughly six weeks apart based on the vendor's current turnaround estimate and the shop's existing order queue. See [[276-tidewatch-print-run-kickoff-meeting]] for the kickoff discussion and [[282-tidewatch-manufacturing-plan]] for vendor scheduling details. Playtesting continues through the fall, both internally on Tuesday evenings and at a couple of local convention demo tables, before the design freezes for print and the approved files go to the vendor for final sign-off ahead of the campaign launch.
