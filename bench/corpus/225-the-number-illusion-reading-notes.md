# The Number Illusion — reading notes

Picked this up after Priyam mentioned it twice. Finished it over a slow weekend.

## Core argument

The author claims your FIRE number is a moving target dressed as fixed. Spreadsheets hide that.

## On "enough"

Chapter three reframes "enough" as a feeling you practice, not a balance you reach. Uncomfortable but true — I keep chasing a number that moves whenever I get close.

## Withdrawal rate skepticism

She's skeptical of the 4% rule, calling it comforting rather than a guarantee for early retirees.

## Favorite passage

"You will not feel richer at two million than at one, if the fear on this path never left." That line stopped me mid-page. It names something I've felt since [[213-early-retirement-doubts]] — the number was never the real problem, my relationship to safety was.

## Where I disagree

She dismisses tracking entirely, which feels extreme. Our [[214-build-a-zero-based-budget]] system isn't anxiety, it's information — dropping it wouldn't remove the fear, just the tool that helps.

## Verdict

Worth the read, especially the middle third. Recommending it to Priyam next, since she asked first.
