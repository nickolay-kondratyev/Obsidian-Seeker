A repeatable checklist for the first booking with a brand new client, built from a few early mistakes made letting this process stay too informal.

## First Contact

Respond within 24 hours even if it's just to confirm receipt and set expectations for a fuller reply, since a fast first response sets the tone for the whole relationship going forward and clients remember it.

## Discovery Call

15-20 minutes, phone or video. Cover product type, volume, timeline, and rough budget.

## Building the Quote

Always request a written brief first, even a rough one, before quoting anything. Use the shot-count method from [[184-how-to-price-a-shoot]], present as a single project total rather than an hourly rate, and include a clear scope boundary so both sides know what triggers an additional charge versus what's covered by the original number quoted.

## Contract and Deposit

Standard contract, 30% deposit before scheduling, balance due on delivery, no exceptions for new clients regardless of how established their own company is.

## Pre-Shoot Prep

Confirm product arrival or studio access two days ahead, share a shot list draft for sign-off, and confirm final headcount if the client plans to attend the shoot in person, since studio space is limited and I need to plan for it.

## Day Of

Walk through the shot list together before shooting starts, adjusting on the spot.

## After Delivery

Send final images with a short note on usage rights and file formats included, and flag the invoice due date clearly rather than assuming the client remembers the terms from the original contract weeks earlier. I also send a short follow-up two weeks after delivery, checking whether the images performed the way they'd hoped, partly out of genuine curiosity and partly because it's a low-pressure opening for a repeat booking without feeling like a sales pitch, since it doubles as useful feedback either way.
