Notes on *The Backcountry Kitchen* by fictional food writer Sana Okafor. Recipes and dehydrating theory both.

## Dehydrating Basics

Chapter one: dehydrating basics, water content and thin slicing. Uniform thickness matters more than I expected.

### Quote

"A dehydrator is a patience machine disguised as a kitchen appliance," (p. 8). True; my first batch of trail chili took eleven hours, not the advertised six.

## Rehydration Ratios

Chapter four covers rehydration ratios by ingredient type. Beans need more water and time than rice.

### Quote

"Underestimate rehydration time and you'll be chewing dinner instead of eating it," (p. 41), a line I now understand painfully well after a memorably crunchy trail dinner last spring. Adding fifteen extra minutes of soak time to everything now, just in case.

## Spice Kits

Chapter six on spice kits is genuinely useful, small pre-mixed packets that add real variety without much extra weight. Stealing this idea for the next trip immediately.

## Verdict

Testing the chili recipe again before it goes on the AT section hike menu.
