Notes on *Reading the High Country* by fictional cartographer Elin Torgersen. Focused on off-trail navigation.

## Contour Lines

Chapter one: contour lines as a language, not decoration. Spacing tells you steepness.

### Quote

"A tight cluster of contour lines is the map warning you before your legs find out the hard way," (p. 12). Relevant for the Sierra High Route planning.

## Handrails

Chapter three covers handrails and catching features. Use a ridge or creek as a guide.

### Quote

"Pick a catching feature before you leave the trail, something you can't walk past without noticing, and you've already solved half of tomorrow's route-finding problem" (p. 29). The most useful line in the book so far.

## Declination

Chapter five on compass declination is dense, lots of math, had to reread it twice before it actually clicked for me. Worth the effort though.

## Verdict

Recommending this to Kofi and Priyam both before our route planning. Dense but genuinely useful.
