Tracking gear purchases and upgrades for this year's trips, mostly to see where the money's actually going instead of guessing. Also useful for deciding what to prioritize next spending when money's tight in a given month.

## Why Track This

Started this after realizing I'd bought three different water filters in two years without ever really comparing them side by side or admitting the first two just weren't working for how I actually hike. This year's budget target is four hundred dollars total across all trip gear, spread across the whole season.

## Running Total

Spent so far: two hundred eighty-four dollars, leaving about one hundred sixteen dollars against the target before the water filter and rain jacket purchases. Both of those are likely to push slightly over budget once tax is included.

## Priorities If Tight

Priorities if the budget gets tight: filter over jacket, since the current jacket still works fine even though it's a bit heavier than I'd like for the Sierra High Route next year. The filter is the one item that's genuinely failed on me before, so it gets first claim on remaining budget.

## Comparing to Last Year

Comparing against last year: total spend was three hundred sixty dollars, mostly on the tent we still use, so this year's number is roughly in line even with a few extra one-off purchases. Notable pattern: I keep underestimating how much boots and shoes cost me annually once you account for replacement cycles, easily the single biggest recurring line item across both years combined. Worth budgeting for that explicitly next year instead of treating each shoe purchase as a surprise expense.
