*Wild Ferments: A Home Cook's Field Notes* by Ingrid Solberg. Borrowed from the library.

## Overview

A practical, occasionally rambling guide to vegetable ferments, built around the author's own kitchen notebook.

## Chapter 2 — Salt ratios

Solberg argues for weighing salt as a percentage of vegetable weight rather than trusting tables, landing on 2% for cabbage and closer to 3.5% for watery vegetables like cucumber. p.34.

## Chapter 4 — Wild capture

Covers catching wild yeast from garden herbs rather than flour, mixed results she admits on p.61.

## Chapter 6 — Troubleshooting

The most useful chapter by far, a symptom-to-cause table for slimy brine, pink kahm-like film, and mushy texture. She's clear pink or fuzzy growth means toss it, while white kahm yeast is annoying but not dangerous.

## Favorite quotes

"Salt is not a preservative, it's a referee, it decides which microbes get to play." p.12. "A ferment that smells wrong will not smell right by day ten." p.45.
