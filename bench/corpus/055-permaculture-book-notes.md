Notes on *Small Yard, Full Plate*, a permaculture-adjacent book Tomasz recommended after the garden meeting.

## Overall Take

Less dogmatic than most permaculture writing I've tried. Practical for a small suburban lot like ours.

## Guilds

Companion planting gets a full chapter, backing up what Tomasz said about comfrey under our apple tree, plus a good bit on alliums I hadn't considered before.

## Water

Swales and rain gardens covered well, directly relevant to our drainage fix.

## Soil

Shortest chapter, mostly a summary pointing elsewhere. A little disappointing given how central soil is to everything else in the book, and I'd hoped for something more substantial given the author's reputation for depth on other topics covered earlier.

## Quote Worth Keeping

"A guild is a community of plants helping each other do what none could do alone." Page 61, worth remembering for the orchard.
