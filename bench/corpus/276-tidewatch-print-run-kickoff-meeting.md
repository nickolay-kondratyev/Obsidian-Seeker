# Print Run Kickoff Meeting

Attendees: Dana, Lotte, Tomasz, Kofi. Purpose: agree on a target print quantity and rough timeline for the first Tidewatch run before Lotte finalizes the Kickstarter budget in [[287-tidewatch-budget-doc]]. Tomasz brought quotes from two vendors he's worked with before on smaller runs for other hobby projects, both able to handle the box size and card stock the team wants, and both quoting comparable per-unit costs at the volumes under discussion, which made this mostly a conversation about quantity and risk tolerance rather than a real choice between vendors themselves. Kofi opened by summarizing feedback from the last two rounds of convention demos so the group had recent sell-through numbers in front of them before debating anything else.

## Quantity Discussion

Tomasz laid out three tiers: three hundred units, five hundred units, and eight hundred units, with per-unit cost dropping meaningfully at each step up, as expected for offset printing at this scale. Lotte's rough Kickstarter modeling suggests three hundred backers is a realistic conservative target based on comparable campaigns she's studied over the past few months, with five hundred as an optimistic but not unreasonable stretch if the campaign gets picked up by a couple of hobby press outlets close to launch. Dana pushed back on ordering above what the campaign actually funds, worried about sitting on unsold inventory in someone's garage for a year, which nobody around the table particularly wants to volunteer for given how small everyone's apartments already are. Kofi raised a middle option: print three hundred fifty as a base run, banking on modest walk-in sales at conventions beyond the Kickstarter backers themselves, since the team already has [[299-how-to-run-convention-demo-table]] planned as a recurring activity through next year regardless of how the campaign performs on its own. The group generally agreed the eight-hundred tier only makes sense if the campaign clearly overfunds partway through, at which point a reprint becomes the safer option anyway rather than gambling on the high tier before a single pledge has come in. Tomasz noted the vendor's minimum order size sits comfortably below even the three-hundred-unit floor, so there's no hard constraint pushing the number up beyond what the group actually wants to commit to this year.

## Decisions

After going back and forth for a good while, the group settled on a base order of three hundred fifty units, matching Kofi's proposal, with an explicit plan to negotiate a reprint at the same per-unit rate if the campaign funds past four hundred backers before the pledge deadline closes. Tomasz will confirm with the vendor whether the reprint rate can be locked in now, in writing, rather than renegotiated later when the team has less leverage and a tighter timeline before the promised delivery window to backers. Lotte will build the Kickstarter budget around the three-hundred-fifty baseline, treating the reprint as a stretch scenario rather than a guaranteed cost in the initial numbers she shares publicly with backers on the campaign page. Dana signed off on this as the number that lets the team sleep at night regardless of how the campaign performs, which was honestly the most important criterion discussed all meeting, more than any of the spreadsheet math that came before it or the vendor's tiered pricing sheet. Kofi will also start tracking convention sell-through estimates more carefully from here on, using notes from [[278-tidewatch-journal-convention-demo]] as a baseline, so the three-fifty number has real data behind it by the time reprint decisions actually need to get made, rather than another round of guesswork under deadline pressure with backers already waiting on shipping updates. The group also agreed that any decision to trigger a reprint should require sign-off from at least two of Dana, Lotte, and Tomasz rather than one person acting alone under time pressure, since a wrong call here carries real financial weight for a self-funded team without a company or investor behind it to absorb a mistake. Lotte suggested a simple shared checklist for the reprint trigger, tied directly to the funding total crossing four hundred backers with at least ten days left before the vendor's cutoff for guaranteeing the original delivery window, so nobody has to make the call from memory under stress.

## Risks

A few risks came up that didn't change the decision but are worth tracking. Tomasz flagged that vendor lead times can stretch during peak season, which could push delivery past the promised window if the team isn't careful about buffer. Lotte worried a slow-funding campaign might leave the team short of even the three-hundred-fifty baseline, in which case the reprint conversation becomes a downsizing conversation instead, something nobody has really planned for yet. Dana wants a fallback plan drafted before launch so the team isn't improvising under pressure if the first week of the campaign underperforms expectations. Kofi raised a smaller risk too: the current component list in [[277-tidewatch-component-list]] still has a placeholder count for storm cards pending the outcome of Dana's deck-size experiments, and locking a print quantity before that number settles could mean reprinting proof files rather than just reordering more boxes, a meaningfully more expensive mistake. The group agreed to hold final production files until the storm deck size is confirmed, even if that slightly delays sending files to the vendor, since a wrong card count is far costlier to fix after the fact than a short wait now. Tomasz also mentioned shipping costs for backers overseas remain unquoted, and Lotte will need real numbers before the budget doc can be considered final rather than a rough draft she keeps caveating in every meeting. Nobody wants to promise a shipping estimate to backers that turns out to be wrong once real freight quotes come back from the vendor.

## Next Steps

Tomasz to get the reprint rate in writing from the vendor by the end of next week and share it with the group before Lotte locks the budget numbers for the campaign page. Lotte to draft the budget doc using the agreed baseline and circulate it for review before the Kickstarter prep meeting later this month, along with a rough underfunding fallback. Dana and Kofi to keep an eye on playtest feedback for anything that might delay the print timeline, since a rules change discovered too late would push proofs back further than anyone wants to explain to backers who already pledged their money in good faith. Kofi to confirm the storm deck's final card count before Tomasz sends anything to the vendor for production, coordinating directly with Dana's design journal so nobody is working from stale numbers. Tomasz to request overseas shipping quotes from the vendor's usual freight partner so Lotte has real figures rather than a placeholder guess in the budget doc. Everyone to reconvene in two weeks once these open threads have answers, rather than letting the print timeline drift without a checkpoint.
