Exploring a formal referral partnership with a local print shop that already handles fulfillment for two of my furniture clients. The idea came up over coffee with their owner, and it seems like a natural fit worth pursuing properly rather than leaving as a vague, casual conversation. Their clients need product photography before anything gets printed, and my clients often need print output after the shoot wraps, so referrals could genuinely flow both directions instead of the one-sided arrangement most partnerships turn into.

## The Opportunity

Right now, referrals between us happen informally and rarely, maybe two or three a year, purely by accident when a client mentions needing the other service in passing. A formal arrangement, even a shared referral sheet handed to new clients, could probably double that without either of us doing much extra work beyond the initial setup. The print shop's owner mentioned they get asked about photography "constantly" from newer catalog clients without one lined up, and I regularly get asked for print recommendations by clients who don't know where to look. There's also a longer-term possibility of bundling: a combined photography-plus-print package for new businesses launching their first product line, priced as one line item, appealing to a founder who doesn't want two vendor relationships during an already stressful launch.

## What a Formal Arrangement Would Look Like

Simplest version: we each keep a one-page flyer or card for the other's business, handed out at the natural moment in client conversations, no money changing hands, just mutual goodwill and reciprocal referrals. A step up would be a modest referral fee, maybe five or ten percent of the first invoice, though this adds real bookkeeping overhead for what might still be a small number of referrals per quarter. The bundled package idea is the most ambitious version and probably a phase-two conversation once we've seen whether the simple referral flow produces real results, rather than jumping straight to a joint offering neither of us has tested informally first. I lean toward starting with the flyer exchange for at least the first quarter, treating it explicitly as a trial, and only proposing the fee structure or bundle once we have real numbers instead of a hopeful guess about how much cross-referral traffic actually exists. Rushing to a formal commission agreement before either of us has data feels like the kind of thing that could sour a promising relationship over a disagreement better information would have avoided.

## Open Questions

Need to figure out how we'd track which referrals came from which direction, since without tracking, a fee structure becomes unenforceable and awkward to raise later. Also unclear whether their regular photographer, if they have one, would feel threatened by this, worth asking directly and early rather than assuming it's fine and finding out otherwise down the road.

## Next Steps

Meeting again in two weeks to propose the simple flyer-exchange version formally and see if they're interested in a three-month trial before anything more structured. Bringing a rough one-page flyer draft to that meeting so we can react to something concrete together instead of talking in the abstract about a vague possibility that's easy to agree with in principle but hard to act on without something tangible in front of us. Also want to sketch a simple shared tracking sheet before the meeting, even a basic spreadsheet both of us update manually, so the tracking question from the open questions above has at least a starting answer rather than another open loop to resolve later, and so the three-month trial actually produces numbers worth acting on.
