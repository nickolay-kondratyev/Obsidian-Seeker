Notes on *The Craftsman Bungalow Restored*, a slim 1998 guide Dana lent me from her renovation.

## Overall Take

Dated in places but the structural chapters on original framing methods hold up well.

## On Original Materials

The author argues old-growth fir framing is denser and more rot-resistant than anything sold today, which matches what Kofi has said about our own studs, still square after sixty years.

## Windows

Chapter four pushes hard against vinyl replacement windows, favoring restoration of the original wood sash instead.

## Kitchens

The kitchen chapter is the most dated part of the book, written before open-plan layouts became the default expectation, so most of its advice assumes a closed galley kitchen much like ours before renovation. Still useful on period-appropriate cabinet proportions, though.

## Quote Worth Keeping

"A bungalow's charm was never in its ornament but in its proportion; get the proportion right and the rest follows." Page 112, worth remembering during the kitchen redo.
