Notes on *The Working Kitchen*, a design guide Priyam's sister recommended before we finalized the layout.

## Overall Take

Practical and specific rather than aspirational. Best chapter is on the work triangle.

## Work Triangle

The classic sink-stove-fridge triangle still holds up as a baseline, the author argues, even in open-plan kitchens where it's tempting to ignore.

## Storage

Chapter six on drawer versus cabinet storage changed how I'm thinking about the pantry pull-outs.

## Lighting

Layered lighting gets a full chapter: ambient, task, and accent treated as genuinely separate design problems rather than one ceiling fixture doing everything at once, which is basically what we had before, and it's already shaping the under-cabinet lighting decision.

## Quote Worth Keeping

"Design the kitchen around how you actually cook, not how a magazine photographs a kitchen." Page 84, underlined twice.
