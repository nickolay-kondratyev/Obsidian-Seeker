## Citation

Voss, *The Eviction Economy: Debt, Displacement, and the Rental Market*, University press, 2023, 210 pages.

## Core Argument

Eviction filings function less as a last resort and more as routine debt collection, p. 14.

## Key Evidence

Filing rates in three metro case studies stayed high even after eviction moratoria ended, correlating with rent-to-income ratios above 40%, pp. 52-61.

## Method Note

Uses court filing records merged with tax assessor data; sample limited to counties with digitized records.

## Useful for Thesis

Directly supports the displacement-risk framing in my methods chapter — filings as a leading indicator rather than a final outcome, worth citing alongside [[157-literature-review-project]] and my own eviction filing data comparison from the conference poster session, pp. 88-94.

## Critique

Doesn't address informal evictions or lease non-renewals at all, which Voss admits in a footnote undercounts true displacement substantially in most cities, p. 130.
