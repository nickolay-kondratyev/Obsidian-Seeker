# Tidewatch Kickstarter Prep

This document tracks everything that needs to be true before the Tidewatch campaign page goes live. The target launch window is early March 2026, timed to land after the final proof approval and before the local convention season picks up. Lotte owns campaign copy and reward tiers, Dana owns component photography, and Tomasz owns the manufacturing quote that backs the pledge math. Nothing goes live until all three sign off on their piece, since a rushed publish date has already caused two false starts on the wording of the front page pitch.

## Reward Tiers

The current draft has four tiers: a five-dollar "digital thanks" tier with a PDF rulebook and a wallpaper set, a base pledge with one copy of Tidewatch and all standard components, a deluxe pledge that upgrades the tide tokens to the acrylic set and adds a cloth map, and a retailer tier for local game shops ordering six or more copies at a discount. Pricing on the deluxe tier is still contested because acrylic tokens add real cost per unit, and Lotte wants final vendor pricing from [[282-tidewatch-manufacturing-plan]] before publishing numbers that can't be walked back once backers start pledging. A rough stretch-goal ladder is sketched but not finalized; ideas floated so far include a scenario expansion card, a second storm-track board, and an alternate sleeve for the lighthouse keeper tokens, though none have a firm cost estimate yet.

## Campaign Page Content

The page needs a short pitch paragraph, a rules overview section, a component gallery, and a FAQ pulled largely from [[289-how-to-write-rulebook-faq]]. Dana is drafting the pitch this week: something like "a cooperative race against rising water where every choice trades speed for safety." The rules overview will lean on short GIFs of the storm track advancing rather than dense paragraphs, since backers skim. Priyam is finishing the hero image, a wide shot of the lighthouse board mid-storm with the tide marker near the top, plus two supporting illustrations contrasting a calm early-game state against a chaotic late-game one. The FAQ draft currently answers eleven questions, mostly about player count flexibility, expected playtime, and whether the game supports solo play, which it does not yet but might as a stretch goal. Kofi is reviewing the rules overview text for accuracy before anything goes public, since backers will hold campaign copy against the eventual printed rulebook if the two disagree, and mismatches erode trust fast once people notice them in the comments during a live campaign, which has happened to other projects the team has followed closely.

## Marketing Assets

Lotte is coordinating a teaser video, three social posts around launch, and a mailing list warm-up for people from last year's demo table. The video uses the same kit from [[279-how-to-pack-playtest-kits]] so components match what backers receive.

> [!tip] Show the real prototype
> Inconsistent prototype photos have burned other campaigns before. Film with kit components, not placeholder art, even if a render would look cleaner.

## Timeline and Open Questions

Copy and reward tiers need to be final by mid-February, two weeks before launch, leaving room for a legal and pricing review; video and photo assets are due the same week, and a soft-launch preview page goes up ten days early to test the pledge widget. Several open questions remain: should the retailer tier stay visible during the campaign or only appear after funding, does the stretch goal ladder commit to a second board before Tomasz confirms print costs at that volume, and does the teaser video include a playtester reacting on camera, since [[283-tidewatch-journal-near-loss]] captured a genuinely funny near-loss moment that might make good footage if someone actually agrees to be filmed for it.
