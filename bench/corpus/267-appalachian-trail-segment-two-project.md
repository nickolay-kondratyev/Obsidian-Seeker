Planning doc for a follow-up AT section next spring, Roan Mountain to Erwin, picking up right where last trip's section left off. About fifty-five miles over four days, slightly shorter than last time.

## Why This Section

Why this section: it connects directly to where we finished, and it includes the famous balds we didn't quite reach last time, plus a resupply option partway through at a small trail town. Kofi's already talking about doing this as an annual spring tradition if this section goes as well as the last one did.

## Daily Plan

Rough daily mileage: fourteen, fifteen, thirteen, thirteen, again camping at shelters rather than carrying a tent, following the same strategy that worked well last time out. One resupply stop at the halfway point, a small store near the trail that Priyam confirmed still stocks basic hiker resupply items as of a recent trip report she found online. Water sources are again frequent according to the guidebook, so carrying capacity stays the same as last section: a liter and a half maximum between refills.

## Shuttle Logistics

Shuttle logistics are simpler this time since we already know the outfitter in the area from the last trip. Booking with them again for both directions, same rough cost as before.

## Gear

Gear stays mostly the same as last section, no tent, bivy as backup, permethrin-treated clothing for ticks again given how effective it was last time. One change: Kofi's replacing his boots with trail runners after seeing how well ours performed, having finally been convinced by three straight days of watching us hike faster and lighter than him.

## Risks

Risks are similar to last time: crowding near popular shelters and balds, fast-changing weather on exposed ridgeline, and ticks, all managed the same way that worked before with no real changes needed to the approach. One new consideration: this section has a higher water crossing partway through that can run faster after heavy rain, so we're checking the forecast closely in the days before departure. If conditions look bad at that crossing, there's a known alternate route around it according to the guidebook, adding about two miles but avoiding the ford entirely.
