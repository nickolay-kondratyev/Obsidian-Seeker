Route planning call for a winter overnight, first cold-weather trip for the three of us together.

## Agenda

Agenda: pick a route with a reliable bailout, confirm avalanche conditions, and settle on the four-season tent versus our usual three-season shelter for this specific trip.

## Discussion

Kofi pulled recent avalanche center reports; conditions are currently rated moderate on the slopes near our planned route, which is manageable if we stay off anything steeper than thirty degrees. Priyam is pushing for a lower valley route instead of the ridge option.

## Decisions

Going with Priyam's valley route; it's more conservative but this is our first winter overnight together and building confidence matters more than an ambitious objective right now. Renting the four-season tent from the outdoor shop rather than buying, since we're not sure yet how much winter camping we'll actually do going forward.

## Open Questions

Still open: whether to bring snowshoes or just microspikes, depending on how much fresh snow falls in the week before the trip, which we won't really know until a couple of days out. Also unresolved is water strategy, since standing water sources may be frozen solid, meaning we might need to melt snow instead, which takes real fuel and time we haven't fully budgeted for yet. Kofi wants to do a gear test night in the backyard before the trip, just to check the sleep system handles the cold.
