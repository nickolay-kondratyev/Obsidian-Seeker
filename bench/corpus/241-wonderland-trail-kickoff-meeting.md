Planning sync for the Wonderland Trail loop, second week of September. Attendees: Dana, Priyam, and Kofi.

## Agenda

Agenda: confirm permit lottery result, review resupply strategy at Longmire, and settle on tent versus tarp. Also decide who drives to the trailhead.

## Discussion

Dana pulled the permit and we got our first-choice itinerary starting at Sunrise. Kofi is pushing for a tarp setup to save weight, Priyam wants the extra bug protection of a full tent given the reports of mosquitoes near Indian Bar.

## Decisions

We're taking Priyam's three-person tent instead of splitting into two shelters; the weight difference is under a pound once split three ways. Resupply happens once, at Longmire on day five, using a bounce bucket Dana will drop off before we start. Kofi is buying the fuel canisters locally instead of shipping them, since stove fuel can't go in the bounce bucket by mail.

## Open Questions

Still open: whether to add the Spray Park alternate on day two, which adds real elevation gain but skips a mile of road walking near Mowich Lake. Also unresolved is whether we start clockwise from Longmire or counterclockwise from Sunrise, since the permit technically allows either direction for this itinerary. Priyam wants to check current snow conditions on the Panhandle Gap crossing before deciding, since a late snow year could make the alternate risky without an ice axe.
