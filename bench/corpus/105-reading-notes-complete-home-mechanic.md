Notes on *The Complete Home Mechanic*, the manual Tomasz lent me for the shed build.

## Premise

A reference manual, not a narrative: covers every common repair a home mechanic needs.

## Key Idea

Most bike problems come from neglected small maintenance rather than dramatic failure: worn cables, dry chains, loose bolts, not sudden catastrophic breaks.

## Quote

"A clean bike is a diagnosed bike" (p. 12) — dirt hides problems.

## Chapter 4 — Drivetrain

The most useful chapter, covering chain wear measurement with a simple ruler trick rather than a dedicated gauge tool. Worth doing monthly on a bike ridden regularly, since a stretched chain quietly wears the cassette too.

## My Take

Dense but genuinely practical, better as a shelf reference than cover-to-cover reading. The diagrams are clearer than most online tutorials I've tried for the same repairs.

## Rating

8/10. Keeping it in the clubhouse once the shed is built.
