Planning doc for a five-day Appalachian Trail section hike next spring, Damascus to Roan Mountain. About seventy miles, moderate terrain by AT standards. First time any of us has hiked this particular stretch of trail together.

## Why This Section

Why this section: it's a well-known highlight, balds with wide views, and manageable logistics with shuttle services running regularly between the two towns during hiking season. Also fits a long weekend plus two vacation days. It's been on Priyam's list for a couple of years and this spring finally lines up for all three of our schedules.

## Daily Plan

Rough daily mileage: fourteen, sixteen, twelve, fifteen, thirteen, camping at established shelters each night rather than carrying a tent, which cuts pack weight noticeably for this specific trip. Shelters on this stretch are well maintained and rarely full outside peak thru-hiker season, so we're comfortable relying on them instead of a backup tent. Water sources are frequent along this section according to the guidebook, so we're not carrying more than a liter and a half at any point between shelters.

## Shuttle Logistics

Shuttle booked both directions through a local outfitter in Damascus, confirmed for the second Friday in May. Cost split three ways comes to about eighteen dollars each. Confirmation email came through last week with a pickup time of seven in the morning.

## Gear Differences

Gear differs from our usual trips mainly in shelter strategy: no tent, just a lightweight bivy each as backup in case a shelter is full or we want privacy on a clear night. Bear canisters aren't required here, just a standard bear hang, which saves real weight versus our usual Pacific Northwest trips.

## Risks

Risks: this section can get crowded with thru-hikers in spring, which affects shelter space and water source lines at popular camps, especially near the balds where everyone stops for the views. Weather on the balds can shift fast, exposed ridgeline with little tree cover for miles at a stretch, so we're watching the forecast closely in the week before departure. Ticks are also a real concern this time of year, so permethrin-treated clothing is going on the gear list for everyone this trip.
