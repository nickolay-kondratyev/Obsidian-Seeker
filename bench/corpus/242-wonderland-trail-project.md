This is the planning document for our nine-day Wonderland Trail circuit around Mount Rainier, starting the second week of September. The goal is a full loop, roughly ninety-three miles with about twenty-two thousand feet of cumulative elevation gain, split across nine hiking days with one built-in weather buffer. Three of us are going: Dana, Priyam, and Kofi, all with prior multi-day experience but none of us have done a loop this long before, so this doc is deliberately over-detailed.

## Itinerary

The permit is confirmed for a clockwise start at Longmire, camping at Devils Dream, Sunset Park, South Puyallup, Golden Lakes, Mowich River, Mystic, Sunrise Camp, and Indian Bar before finishing back at Longmire. Each camp was chosen to keep daily mileage under twelve miles, except day four, which runs closer to fourteen because of limited camping options near Golden Lakes. We built a rest day into the schedule near Sunrise in case of weather or an injury, since that's roughly the trail's halfway point and has the best bailout options. See [[247-appalachian-trail-section-project]] for how we scoped a similar multi-week itinerary last year, which is where most of this planning template comes from.

## Gear and Logistics

Base weight target is fourteen pounds per person before food and water, which we're hitting mostly by sharing the tent, stove, and water filter three ways. Dana is carrying the tent, Priyam has the stove and cook pot, and Kofi is on water filter and first aid duty, rotating who carries the bear canister each day. Food is five days of dehydrated dinners we're making ourselves, plus a resupply box left at the Longmire ranger station covering the back half of the loop. Bear canisters are required in this park regardless of campsite, so we're each bringing one rather than trying to split a single large canister between three packs. Water sources are frequent enough that we're only carrying one liter at a time between camps, refilling and treating at nearly every creek crossing on the route. Footwear is trail runners for Dana and Priyam, boots for Kofi, who prefers the ankle support given a previous sprain on a rocky descent two years ago on wet granite.

## Risks

Biggest risk is river crossings after a hot spell, since snowmelt can turn the Tahoma Creek crossing dangerous by early afternoon. We're crossing that section first thing in the morning to avoid peak meltwater flow. Weather is the other concern, so we're carrying a satellite messenger as backup, since cell coverage is nonexistent for most of the loop.

## Budget

Total estimated cost per person is around three hundred and forty dollars, covering permit fees, the shuttle from Seattle, resupply shipping, and a fuel canister purchase in Ashford. Permit fee was twenty dollars for the reservation plus six dollars per person per night, which came out to roughly seventy-four dollars each for the full loop. Shuttle costs are being split three ways, and we're leaving one car at Longmire and using a hired shuttle back from the trailhead since it's a loop with a single start and end point. Gear purchases this cycle were mostly Kofi's new boots and a replacement water filter cartridge, since the old one clogged badly on a trip in July and never fully recovered its flow rate.
