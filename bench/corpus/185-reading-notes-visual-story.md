Notes from *The Visual Story*, Kofi's copy, borrowed after he raved about chapter four on framing.

## Framing

Nothing revolutionary but a good reminder: negative space is doing work, not just empty background.

## On Reflective Surfaces

Page 112: most beginners light reflective products like they're matte, and it never works. Think of the surface as a mirror aimed at controlled shapes.

## Quote Worth Keeping

"The product doesn't care about your softbox. It only cares what shape it sees."

## My Take

Chapter six has a diagram for a v-flat tent for glass bottles I want to try next week. Some of this overlaps with what I already do for jewelry, but naming it "controlled reflection mapping" is useful. Worth revisiting before [[194-how-to-white-background-setup]].

## Where It Falls Short

The book barely touches pricing or client workflow, it's purely a lighting and composition text, not a substitute for the business-side reading I still need to do.

## Rating

Three and a half stars, worth the borrow, probably not worth buying my own copy.
