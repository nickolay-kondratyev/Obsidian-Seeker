# Tidewatch Budget Doc

This tracks projected costs and funding sources for the Tidewatch print run, kept current by Lotte and reviewed by Dana monthly. It complements [[282-tidewatch-manufacturing-plan]] and should be treated as the source of truth for numbers, not the manufacturing plan.

## Funding Sources

We're funding this through a small Kickstarter-style campaign plus a modest amount of personal contribution from Dana and Lotte as a buffer against shortfalls. Lotte estimates the campaign needs to clear a specific threshold before the print order can be placed with confidence, and that threshold is detailed further down in this document under the manufacturing cost section.

## Manufacturing Costs

Manufacturing is the largest line item by far. Based on Tomasz's vendor quotes referenced in [[282-tidewatch-manufacturing-plan]], a run of roughly three hundred copies lands near the higher end of our estimated per-unit cost, driven mostly by the chipboard board and wooden pawns rather than the cards or rulebook, which price out comparatively cheaply even with the linen finish under consideration for the card stock. Dana wants this figure revisited once the token count from the ongoing balance work settles, since more tokens directly raise the per-unit cost.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping estimates remain rough since we haven't finalized box dimensions yet, but Lotte has budgeted a conservative per-unit figure covering domestic orders, with international shipping treated separately and likely passed through closer to actual cost once real rates come in.

## Marketing Costs

Marketing spend is intentionally modest: a small print ad budget for a hobby publication, some paid boosting of Kickstarter update posts, and a print proof set aside specifically for review copies sent to a few tabletop reviewers Lotte has relationships with from prior projects. Nothing here is locked yet, and Lotte may reallocate between these lines depending on early campaign traction once it launches.

## Contingency Reserve

We're holding back roughly fifteen percent of the total campaign target as an unallocated contingency reserve, informed directly by the reprint-buffer recommendation in [[285-reading-notes-supply-chain-boardgames]]. This reserve covers a failed proof, a damaged shipment, or a mid-run price increase from either vendor without forcing the group to cut scope on the actual game or the components inside the box. Lotte reviews this figure every time a new vendor quote comes in, since raw material shifts could eat into it faster than expected if left unmonitored for too long between updates.

## Next Review

Lotte will update actual figures against these projections once the campaign launches, with the next full budget review scheduled shortly after the funding window closes in [[292-tidewatch-kickstarter-prep]], and Dana will sign off on the numbers before any spending changes are approved.
