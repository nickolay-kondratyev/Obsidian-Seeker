Committee call about the spring bike swap event, kept short since most had work early.

Attendees: Priyam, Kofi, and Dana, dialling in from the usual video call since Tomasz was away for the week visiting family up north.

## Agenda

Decide the date for the spring bike swap, confirm whether the clubhouse yard or the school hall down the road is the better venue this year, and settle who's handling the listing table on the day.

## Decisions

The swap is set for the second Saturday in March, weather permitting, at the school hall rather than the yard, since last year's rain nearly cancelled the whole event with twenty minutes' notice. Kofi will handle bookings for the hall this week, and Dana volunteered to run the listing table again, having done it for the past two years without complaint.

## Discussion

There was a longer conversation about charging a small table fee for sellers this year, to cover the hall rental which has gone up since last spring. Priyam was cautiously in favour, arguing a modest fee wouldn't put off serious sellers and would close most of the budget gap. Dana pushed back, worried it might feel like a departure from the swap's informal spirit. No final decision was made; Priyam will float the idea in the group chat before the next meeting.
