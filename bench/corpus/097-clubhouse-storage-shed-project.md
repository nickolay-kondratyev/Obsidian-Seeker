# Clubhouse Storage Shed Project

The clubhouse back room is bursting with marshal vests, cones, spare tubes, and folding tables, and it's been a mess for two seasons. This document tracks the plan to build a proper storage shed before spring.

## Why Now

We lost a box of marshal vests over the summer, simply misplaced somewhere in the clutter, and had to buy replacements before the autumn hill climb. Committee agreed at the November meeting that this couldn't continue, and set aside part of this year's surplus membership fees specifically for a shed rather than letting it sit unspent again.

## Design

After looking at three options, we're going with a 3x2 metre timber-framed shed with a felt roof, wide double doors for wheeling bikes or tables in and out, and simple shelving down one wall for smaller items. Tomasz sketched a layout that keeps the floor clear in the middle for moving the folding tables without having to lift them over anything. We priced a metal kit shed as an alternative but timber matched the clubhouse better and wasn't meaningfully more expensive once delivery was included.

## Budget

Materials and a flat-pack kit come to roughly £1,400, with another £200 set aside for a concrete base and gravel. The club has £1,100 earmarked already; the shortfall will come from a small raffle at the spring social.

## Timeline

Base laid in February once the ground firms up, kit delivered and assembled over a weekend in early March with volunteer labour, and shelving fitted the following weekend. Target is everything usable well before the spring century needs its marshal kit organised, boxed, and ready to load into cars on the morning of the event.

## Risks

The main risk is the base: if the ground is still too wet in February the concrete pour slips, which pushes the whole timeline back by weeks rather than days, since we can't easily book the volunteer labour twice. Planning permission isn't required for a structure this size under current rules, but Priyam is double-checking with the landowner in writing anyway, since the clubhouse itself is on a long lease and we don't want any ambiguity about what we're allowed to build on the side yard.

## Next Steps

Confirm the concrete supplier by end of January, finalise the volunteer labour list for the assembly weekend, and get written confirmation from the landowner before anything gets ordered or delivered to the site.
