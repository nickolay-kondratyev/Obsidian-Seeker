*Ferment Zine*, issue 3, picked up at the swap meet from an unminded table.

## Overview

Photocopied, stapled, a dozen short pieces from different home fermenters rather than one voice.

## Standout piece

A short essay on failed batches was the best thing here, more honest than most blogs.

> [!note] Quoted from the zine
> "Nobody photographs the jar that grew fuzz."

## Recipe worth trying

A quick pickled mustard seed recipe, half a page, looks simple enough to try this weekend.

## Weakest section

An interview transcript ran too long and repeated points already made earlier in the issue, apparently unedited beyond stapling it straight into the layout, which dragged the pacing of an otherwise short and readable zine noticeably by the final page.

## Overall impression

Rough production but genuine voice throughout, worth seeking out issue 4 if [[141-equipment-swap-meet-meeting]] happens again and more copies turn up there too.

## Would read again

Yes, and I'll look for the table next time instead of finding it by accident.
