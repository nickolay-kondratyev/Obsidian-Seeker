Trailhead logistics call for the Appalachian Trail section hike, six weeks out.

## Agenda

Agenda: confirm car shuttle versus paid shuttle, parking situation at Damascus, and whether we need a permit for this section at all.

## Discussion

No permit required for this stretch, confirmed by Priyam with the ranger district office directly last week. Parking at Damascus is free but limited on weekends, so we're arriving Thursday night instead of Friday morning to secure a spot.

## Decisions

Decided on the paid shuttle instead of a car shuttle, since it's only eighteen dollars each and saves us from leaving a car unattended at a trailhead for five days. Kofi booked it directly with the outfitter for the second Friday in May, seven in the morning pickup from the hotel in Damascus.

## Communication Plan

Cell coverage is spotty to nonexistent for most of the route, confirmed by a trip report Priyam found describing long stretches with no signal between towns. This makes the satellite messenger more important than planned, so we're renting one from the same outfitter as the shuttle. Dana is setting up a nightly check-in message to her partner, our emergency contact for the trip. We also talked through a rough bailout plan in case of injury near the road crossings partway through the route.
