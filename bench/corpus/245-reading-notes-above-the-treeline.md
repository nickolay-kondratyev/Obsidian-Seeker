Notes on *Above the Treeline* by a fictional alpine guide, Mara Voss.

## Weather Reading

Chapter two: weather reading in the alpine zone. Cloud shapes over ridgelines predict storms hours out.

### Quote

"A ridge that looks friendly at nine in the morning can be a lightning trap by one," (p. 34). Ties directly to the Panhandle Gap timing question from [[241-wonderland-trail-kickoff-meeting]].

## River Crossings

Chapter four covers river crossings, mostly technique over gear. Face upstream, unclip your hip belt first.

### Quote

"Unclip before you cross, every time, even the shallow ones," she repeats three separate times across two chapters (p. 61, p. 88). Voss is emphatic about this because a pack full of water can drown you faster than the current itself.

## Gear Chapter

Chapter seven is weaker, a grab bag of gear opinions that feel dated, boots and cotton mostly, though the base layer section holds up fine. Skimmed most of it.

## Verdict

Overall: worth it for chapters two and four alone. Recommending to Kofi before our trip.
