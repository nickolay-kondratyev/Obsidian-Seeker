# Reading Notes: "Small-Batch Tabletop, Big-Batch Problems"

Independent games-industry newsletter, pages 4-9. Covers print runs under a thousand units, close to Tidewatch's scale.

## Core Claim

Small runs pay a steep per-unit premium since tooling and minimums don't scale down, hurting margins.

## Vendor Minimums

Page 5 lists typical minimums by component type; most chipboard and card vendors won't go below five hundred units without a surcharge.

## Sample Cost Table

## Freight Warning

Page 7 warns that ocean freight delays of four to six weeks are now common and should be built into any launch timeline as a default assumption, not an edge case worth ignoring only when it happens to us.

## Author's Recommendation

Order ten to fifteen percent over target quantity to absorb damaged units and reprint buffer, since reordering a short run later costs far more per unit.
