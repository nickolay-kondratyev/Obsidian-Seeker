Notes on *Fuel and Distance*, a nutrition guide Kofi swears by for long rides.

## Premise

Most cyclists underfuel long rides, then wonder why the last hour feels terrible.

## Key Idea

Aim for 60-90 grams of carbohydrate per hour on rides over ninety minutes, split between fluids and solid food rather than one or the other alone.

## Quote

"Bonking is a planning failure, not bad luck" (p. 33).

## Chapter 5 — Hydration

Covers sweat rate testing, weighing yourself before and after a ride to estimate fluid loss. Did this once on a hot July ride and lost nearly a kilogram, which changed how seriously I take bottle refills.

## My Take

More rigorous than most cycling nutrition content online, backed by actual studies rather than anecdote. A bit heavy on numbers, but genuinely useful for anyone training seriously.

## Rating

8/10. Changed how I pack food for the century ride this year.
