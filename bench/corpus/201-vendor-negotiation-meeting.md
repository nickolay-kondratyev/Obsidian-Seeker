Call with the studio's backup-storage vendor about renewing at a lower tier. 25 minutes.

## Agenda

Review current storage usage against the plan tier, and see whether a smaller tier fits now that the archive policy from [[197-backup-workflow-project]] is in place.

## Current Usage

Account rep pulled actual usage: I'm at 62% of the current 2TB tier, well under the ceiling, and haven't been near the limit in eight months, which surprised me given how much footage I thought I was storing.

## The Negotiation

I asked directly whether a 1TB tier would cover projected usage for the next year given the new retention and cold-storage archiving policy, which should reduce active storage need further. The rep confirmed 1TB would fit with room to spare, and offered a locked-in annual rate for a twelve-month commit, saving roughly 15%.

## Outcome

Downgraded to the 1TB annual plan effective next billing cycle, saving about $9/month, small but real given how many small recurring costs add up quietly over a year. Confirmed the migration between tiers is automatic and requires no action on my end, no re-upload or manual file move needed, which the rep confirmed before I agreed to anything, and I asked twice just to be sure since a botched migration would be a bad surprise to discover later.
