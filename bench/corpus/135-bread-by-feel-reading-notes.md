*Bread by Feel* by Anders Vik. A gift from Lotte after my flat-loaf phase last winter.

## Overview

Argues against measuring everything, pushing readers toward touch and sight over strict timers and scales.

## Chapter 1 — Distrust the timer

Vik insists bulk fermentation should end on dough behavior, not the clock, echoing what I'd already learned the hard way. p.9-14 covers the jiggle test in useful detail.

## Chapter 3 — Hands, not gadgets

Skeptical of lames and bannetons, arguing a towel and a sharp knife are plenty. p.28.

## Chapter 5 — Shaping by tension

The best chapter, matching almost exactly what I landed on in [[134-shaping-scoring-sourdough-howto]] after months of trial and error. Vik describes the taut-skin feeling as "a drum, not a balloon," a better image than anything else I've read. p.61-67.

## Favorite quotes

"A scale tells you what went in. Your hands tell you what actually happened." p.11. Refreshingly opinionated for a bread book overall, more than most.

## Verdict

Good complement to more precise sources, not a replacement for any of them.
