# Tidewatch Distribution Plan

This document outlines how finished copies move from the print vendor to backers and local shops after the Kickstarter campaign funds. It covers fulfillment tiers, the local retailer track, and rough timing, and is meant to be read alongside [[291-tidewatch-logistics-sync]] and [[282-tidewatch-manufacturing-plan]].

## Fulfillment Tiers

Backers fall into two fulfillment groups: direct-to-door shipping for individual pledges, and bulk pallet delivery for the retailer tier, routed through the local game shop network instead of individual mailboxes. Direct shipping uses the single courier Lotte and Tomasz selected during the logistics sync, covering the first wave of roughly two hundred units domestically, with international shipping handled as a later phase.

## Retailer Track

Local game shops that pledge at the retailer tier receive a single bulk shipment per shop rather than individual boxes, which keeps per-unit shipping cost meaningfully lower for both shop and project. Shops handle their own local pickup or delivery arrangements with customers who preorder through them directly. Tomasz is the point of contact for retailer questions, since he already has a relationship with the print vendor and a working sense of freight timing for pallet-sized shipments.

## Timing

Fulfillment begins roughly six weeks after the campaign closes, assuming the print run finishes on schedule and customs clearance goes smoothly. Domestic backers ship first, retailers second, international backers last, since customs adds the most delay.

## Tracking and Communication

Backers receive a shipping notification with tracking as soon as their box leaves the fulfillment center, generated automatically from the courier's system rather than sent manually. Lotte will post a fulfillment status update roughly every two weeks during the shipping window so backers aren't left guessing, since silence during fulfillment is a common source of frustration on other campaigns.

## Damage, Returns, and Open Risks

Any box arriving visibly damaged gets a no-questions-asked replacement, funded from a small contingency in the manufacturing budget for this purpose. Backers report damage through a simple form rather than individual messages, so Lotte can track volume and flag the courier if a pattern suggests transit damage. Separately, the plan assumes the courier can absorb roughly two hundred parcels in a single week without delay, which hasn't been tested at this volume before, and international costs remain a rough estimate rather than a locked quote.

## Next Steps

Lock the domestic courier's volume capacity before campaign launch, get a firm international shipping quote from Tomasz, and confirm the damage-replacement contingency budget against [[287-tidewatch-budget-doc]] figures before this plan is treated as final rather than a working draft still open to revision.
