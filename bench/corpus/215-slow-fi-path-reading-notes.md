## About the book

*The Slow FI Path*, pseudonymous blogger-turned-author. Borrowed from Lotte, finished in about a week.

## Core argument

Optimizing purely for a withdrawal-rate number ignores the journey years; "slow FI" trades speed for sustainability.

## Quotes worth keeping

"Enough is not a number you calculate once, it's a question you keep asking" (p. 34).

## Agreements and disagreements

The waiting-room framing resonated, matching [[213-early-retirement-doubts]] about the plan feeling more real than the life it funds. But the book underweights US healthcare cost volatility, treating it almost as a footnote, and leans on anecdote over data in chapters three and four.

## Takeaways

Worth revisiting the coast FIRE math in [[224-calculate-your-fi-number]] using the book's "enough" framing instead of a bare percentage-of-expenses target this coming quarter.

## Would recommend

Yes, especially to anyone deep in optimization and feeling burned out by it.
