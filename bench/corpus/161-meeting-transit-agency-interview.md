## Key points

Tomasz described the 2023 route restructuring as driven primarily by driver shortages, not demand modeling — a detail not mentioned in any public agency document I've read so far. Service cuts hit low-income corridors hardest, which he acknowledged directly and without prompting, unlike the agency's official messaging around the changes at the time.

## Data and access

He offered to share anonymized boarding data by stop for 2022-2025, pending a data-sharing agreement through the agency's research office. Said turnaround is usually about three weeks once the request is filed, sometimes longer if legal has to review it first, which I should plan around when scheduling the next stage of analysis. Also mentioned a colleague, Lotte, who handles GTFS feed maintenance and could speak to historical schedule changes if the boarding data alone doesn't explain the ridership patterns I'm trying to account for in the restructuring chapter.
