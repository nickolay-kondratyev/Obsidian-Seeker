# E-Bike Loaner Fleet

A small pilot fleet of two e-bikes members can borrow for a week at a time, aimed at people curious whether an e-bike would help them keep up on club rides without committing to a purchase upfront.

## Why

Several older members have mentioned struggling to keep pace on hillier routes and considering an e-bike, but none wanted to spend over two thousand pounds without trying one properly first. Committee agreed a small loaner fleet removes that barrier and might keep those members riding with the club rather than drifting away.

## Fleet Details

Two hybrid e-bikes, mid-range models chosen for reliability over performance, bought secondhand from a dealer clearing demo stock at a significant discount. Both come with a spare battery, a charger, and a basic lock, stored at the clubhouse once the storage shed is finished. Loans run one week at a time, booked through the group chat until the new website's booking system goes live, at which point this will move there like everything else.

## Cost

£2,400 total for both bikes plus spares, funded from the same surplus discussed at the AGM. Ongoing costs are minor: occasional servicing and battery replacement every few years, budgeted separately going forward each year.

## Insurance and Liability

The club's existing insurance policy needed a small rider added specifically for loaned e-bikes, confirmed with the insurer before the bikes were even ordered. Borrowers sign a simple agreement acknowledging responsibility for damage beyond normal wear, similar to how the loaner helmets for the youth programme are handled already.

## Booking Rules

Members can borrow one bike per household per month, capped so the two bikes reach as many curious members as possible rather than becoming one person's semi-permanent second bike. Priority goes to members trying an e-bike for the first time over repeat borrowers, and borrowers are asked to leave brief feedback afterward covering fit, range, and whether it changed their thinking about buying one, which will inform whether the fleet expands next year.

> [!tip] Try before you buy
> Both loaner e-bikes are available now — message the group chat to book a week.

So far three members have borrowed a bike, and one has already ordered their own as a direct result.
