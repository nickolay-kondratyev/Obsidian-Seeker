# Home button doesn't resume from standby

I recently had a problem with the microUSB charging port on my Samsung Galaxy Note 1 and got it replaced. Since then, I notice an odd change in behaviour: when the phone is sleeping (ie, black screen), pushing the home button (at the bottom) doesn't wake it up. Pushing the power button (on the side) does wake it up. Questions:   * is this a software change that just happened to coincide with my hardware issue?   * is there some plausible connection between those events?   * is it a bad thing? (I can't really decide - on the plus side, less chance of phone waking in my pocket.)
